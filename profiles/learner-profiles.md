---
title: 'Learner Profiles'
---

The intended audience is library data-services staff, OSPO and DataSquad students,
and incoming service operators. These profiles are teaching assumptions to check
in a pilot, not observations about named colleagues.

## Student contributor

Assume basic terminal navigation, text editing, and Git familiarity, but no AWS
account or administrator background. The learner wants to publish collection
content and understand a failure without guessing. Success is a local published
page, an explicit verification check, and a reproducible repair. Provide extra
setup support for containers and YAML indentation.

## Staff member or incoming operator

Assume familiarity with a service's users and operational consequences, with
uneven experience in automation. The learner needs to distinguish desired state,
reported changes, application correctness, and recoverable data. The public core
builds those habits; the Dataverse extension supplies operational reasoning.
Production competence requires separate authorized practice and a current runbook.

## Certification learner

Assume this learner also has, or is developing, Linux administration experience.
They need to write automation from requirements without a finished role or AI
solution. The optional certification page distinguishes executed drills from
mapped objectives and planned VM practice. Containers do not establish SSH,
real-host SELinux/firewall behavior, or persistence after reboot.
